# Arm P0: run plan (05 §2, D29; doubling estimate D49)

- **Status:** plan, not launched. Before launch it needs the tuning allowance written down (below)
  and the user's go-ahead for the spend (≈ $200 at measured prices).
- **Arm:** P0 = the released policy recipe plus the D37/D46 correctness fixes (`05` §2.1, §2.4).
  Config `experiments/configs/p0.toml`; initialization a copy of pilot A
  (`workdir/pilot/qs-a/pilot-qs-a-final.pth`, `15121432…`); 1 seed (the reference arm).
- **Fixed for Phase 1:** obs v4 (D49), GRP-v2, u = [0.9, 0.5, −0.1, −1.3], torch 2.6 on RTX 4090s
  (D50), and the private network over SSH tunnels (D48).
- **Labels:** quick-model evidence (D41). Every lab row is exploratory.

## Compute and layout

- **Budget:** 3 boxes × 6 days = **432 billed box-hours** (`05` §2.2). That's 1 learner + 2 actor
  boxes, counted by `fleet.py` over every box the run rents, failed attempts and replacements
  included.
- **Launch** (from a clean commit):

  ```bash
  F="python3 tools/fleet/fleet.py"
  $F up --run p0-a --config experiments/configs/p0.toml --init workdir/pilot/qs-a/pilot-qs-a-final.pth \
      --actors 2 --procs <tuning allowance> --hard-hours 156 --verify --yes
  setsid -f $F watch --run p0-a --replace --budget-box-hours 432 --snapshot-at 0.25,0.5 \
      > workdir/fleet/runs/p0-a/watch.out 2>&1 < /dev/null
  ```

  `watch` freezes `p25` and `p50` at 108 and 216 box-hours, then `full` at 432, and tears the
  fleet down. The watchdog (156 h ≈ 6.5 days) is the backstop if `watch` dies.
- **Tuning allowance (open, to be written here before launch; ≤ 1 box-day, `05` §2.2):**
  - actor batch size: 800 vs 2,000 games (+28% actor rate, policy lag mean ~2, max 4;
    `experiments/fleet-host-benchmarks.md`);
  - processes per actor box: 2 or 3.

## Evaluations (lab, family B, after the run)

1. **G0 selection (`05` §2.3):** `python -m lab run --test T-std --challenger …/snapshots/full.pth
   --champion workdir/pilot/qs-a/pilot-qs-a-final.pth --arm P0 --horizon K`.
2. **Doubling estimate (D49), pre-registered here:**
   - **Measurement:** `python -m lab measure --sets 100000 --challenger …/snapshots/full.pth
     --champion …/snapshots/p50.pth --arm P0 --horizon K`. That's fixed N on family B; the result
     is Δu with a bootstrap 95% CI.
   - **Size:** σ_set ≈ 0.33 (D44), so 100K sets give a CI half-width of ≈ 0.002 u. On the
     workstation that's ≈ 15 h (20K sets took 2.9 h in the SP ablation); the workstation must not
     train meanwhile.
   - **Reading,** against v5's break-even of 0.002–0.004 u per doubling
     (`experiments/sp-ablation.md` §7):
     - CI lower bound > 0.004: one doubling clearly pays for v5's extra games. The G0 review
       weighs a switch.
     - CI upper bound < 0.002: extra games don't pay for the SP features. v4 stays unless the D31
       curves say otherwise.
     - Otherwise grey: the D31 actor-size curves (6 vs 12 box-days) decide at G0.
   - **Not a verdict on the arm.** It measures what the second half of this run's compute bought.
3. **Exploratory, only if lab time allows:** `p50` vs `p25` (the doubling before), for the curve's
   shape. Same command and size.

## Caveats, stated before the result

- **One run.** The ½ → full gain is this run's. Training luck (~±0.003 u per network in the SP
  ablation, `sp-ablation.md` §6) partly cancels within a run, since both checkpoints share the
  first half of it, but the gain itself still varies between runs.
- **Quick model.** It starts from a b8c192 BC student. Gains per doubling from a stronger start
  may be smaller.
- **Engine, not mahjong.** If P0 doesn't learn (Equim's early PPO didn't, `01` §2), the estimate
  is ≈ 0 for this engine. That says nothing about RL in general, and P1/V/H supply their own
  checkpoints (`05` §2.2).
