## B0 warm-up (05 §4), per seed; outside the 432 box-hours (D53)

- **What:** the policy is frozen at the seed's pilot, and a fresh critic learns from that pilot's
  games (`--set critic.warmup=true`).
- **Budget, pre-registered:** **12 billed box-hours.** At the learner's ≈ 110K hanchan/h that is
  ≈ 0.4M hanchan, inside `05` §4's 0.3–0.5M. `watch --budget-box-hours 12` takes the `full`
  snapshot, which becomes the P1 run's `--init`, and downs the fleet.
- **Acceptance, judged on the trainer's diagnostics over the last third of B0:**
  1. calibration (10 bins) monotone in the final window;
  2. explained variance overall and in the last kyoku (`critic/explained_variance_last_kyoku`)
     not falling. Plateaued or rising both count.
- **If either fails:** extend once by 4 box-hours, continuing from the snapshot. If it still
  fails, stop and report: D52's reopen condition.
- Launch, seed 1 (seed 2 the same with `p1-b.toml`, pilot B and `p1b-b0`):

  ```bash
  F="python3 tools/fleet/fleet.py"
  $F up --run p1a-b0 --config experiments/configs/p1.toml --init workdir/pilot/qs-a/pilot-qs-a-final.pth \
      --actors 2 --procs 2 --set dataset.num_workers=4 --set critic.warmup=true --hard-hours 8 --verify --yes
  setsid -f $F watch --run p1a-b0 --replace --allow-zen2 --budget-box-hours 12 --snapshot-at 0.5 \
      --max-dollars 20 > workdir/fleet/runs/p1a-b0/watch.out 2>&1 < /dev/null
  ```

- **Seed 1's learner host died at the budget** (`instance exited`), so its `full` snapshot
  (step 216,800) is the last pulled checkpoint and may miss up to ~30 min of the end. The watcher
  then rented a replacement learner it didn't need (0 drains), which went down with the run.
- **The final-window calibration test is noisy:** only about a third of the last-third windows
  are monotone, in both seeds. Seed 2 passing and seed 1 failing it is close to chance. The rule
  stands as registered; the noise is reported here.
